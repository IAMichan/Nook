Privacy

Nook in your browser: privacy policy

Nook's browser extension connects your browser to the Nook app on your own Mac. It has no server of its own, no analytics and no tracking.

What the extension handles

  • Page content. The text, fields and buttons of a page, and a screenshot of the visible tab, only when you ask Nook about that page or give Nook a task in your browser.
  • Your tabs. The titles and addresses of your open tabs, when Nook is asked which tabs are open.
  • Your settings. The sites where you switched Nook off, and whether Nook is paused. These are stored in your browser.
  • The side panel. Your conversation in the side panel and the list of what Nook did, kept only for the current browser session.

Where it goes

Everything the extension reads goes only to the Nook app on your Mac, through the browser's native messaging. The extension sends nothing to the developer or to any server.

Nook answers with the model you chose in Nook. By default that is a model on your own Mac. If you chose a cloud model in Nook's settings, Nook sends your question and the page content it needs to that provider to answer you, under that provider's terms. With a privacy level that keeps personal sources on this Mac, a cloud model or a coding agent does not get Nook's browser tools at all. Read more about models and privacy levels on Nook's privacy page.

What it never does

  • It never types into password fields and never reads what is in them.
  • It never sells data, and never uses it for advertising or for anything other than answering you and carrying out the tasks you give.
  • It runs nothing on a page until you ask. Every action (opening a page, clicking, typing, pressing a key, closing a tab) waits for your approval in Nook first and is written to Nook's audit log.
  • It never touches the browser's own pages, local files or the web stores.
  • It loads no code from the internet: everything it runs ships inside the extension.

Your control

In the side panel you can switch Nook off for any site, or pause it in the whole browser. To stop it completely, click Disconnect in Nook › Settings › Editor & browser, or remove the extension from your browser. Removing it also removes the settings it stored.

Open source

The extension's code is public in apps/browser-extension, and how it works is described in BROWSER-EXTENSION.md.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub. Security issues: report them privately as described in the security policy.
