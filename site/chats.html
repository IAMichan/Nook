Questions

Good to know.

Does Nook send messages by itself?

No. @nook only writes the message into the field. You read it, change it if you like, and send it yourself.

Which apps can I answer in Nook itself?

WhatsApp, Telegram, and every app Nook's chat helper links, such as Instagram, Messenger and Signal. For the others, ⌘ Return copies your answer and opens the chat in its own app.

Where are my chats stored?

On your Mac. Collecting needs Full Disk Access, is off by default, and looks back two weeks the first time you turn it on.

What does @nook look at when it writes?

The newest messages of the chat, and when it helps your calendar, mail and other chats. You see each place it looked as a small chip above the draft.

Can I keep using Beeper?

You can, and you can also move over: Settings › Chat apps › Moving from Beeper brings your linked apps, chats with recent history, and pinned, muted and archived chats along. Each app is then linked once more in Nook.
